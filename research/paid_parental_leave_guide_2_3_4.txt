2.3.4 Historical income limits for PPL
Summary

This topic contains historical income limits for the individual PPL income limit (1.1.I.55) and the family PPL income limit (1.1.F.10).

The tables below show the dates the income limit was indexed, as well as the reference income year.

Individual PPL income limit
Year applied from	Reference income year	Income limit ($)
01/01/2011	2019–2020 and prior	150,000
01/07/2021	2020–21	151,350
01/07/2022	2021–22	156,647
01/07/2023	2022–23	168,865
01/07/2024	2023–24	175,788
01/07/2025	2024–25	180,007
01/07/2026	2025–26	186,487
Family PPL income limit
Year applied from	Reference income year	Income limit ($)
01/07/2023	2021–22
2022–23	350,000
01/07/2024	2023–24	364,350
01/07/2025	2024–25	373,094
01/07/2026	2025–26	386,525

Act reference: PPLAct section 41 The individual PPL income limit and family PPL income limit

Policy reference: PPL Guide 2.3.3 Indexation of PPL income limits for PLP
