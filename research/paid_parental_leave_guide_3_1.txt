3.1 Current rate of PLP
Introduction

The rate of payment for PLP is based on the hourly rate of the NMW.

Each flexible PPL day (1.1.F.70) that is payable to a person is paid at the daily NMW amount (1.1.D.10) for PLP. The daily NMW amount for PLP is 7.6 times the hourly rate of the NMW.

From 1 July 2026, the current hourly NMW amount is $26.44.

The current daily NMW amount for PLP is $200.94 ($26.44 × 7.6, rounded).

The current weekly rate of PLP, derived from the daily NMW amount, is $1,004.70 ($200.94 × 5).

Where a flexible PPL day falls on 1 July, the daily NMW amount is increased on that day.

PLP is a taxable payment.

Act reference: PPLAct section 11D Flexible PPL day, section 65 The amount of an instalment
