4.10 Historical income & assets limits & information
Introduction

This chapter contains historical information on income and assets test and limits.

Notes to the various tables are included immediately after the relevant table.

Act reference: SSAct section 8(1)-'income', section 11(1)-'asset'

Policy reference: SS Guide 4.3 Ordinary income, 4.6 Assets

In this chapter

This chapter contains the following sections:

4.10.2 Historical unemployment (including JSP), sickness benefit & SA income test from September 1969 to present date
